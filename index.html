PROCESSES

Finance processes we automate.

Day-to-day finance

Bookkeeping

Transactions are categorised and reconciled automatically, instead of at month-end review.

Accounts payable

Bills are captured, matched to the right vendor and account, and routed for approval.

Accounts receivable

Outstanding invoices are tracked automatically, with reminders sent on a schedule you set.

Payroll

Pay runs are processed on a fixed schedule, without re-entering the same data every cycle.

Reporting & planning

Financial reporting

Statements are generated straight from your books on schedule, ready for review.

Dashboards

Revenue, cash, AR and AP are pulled into one live view that updates automatically.

Budgeting

Actuals are tracked against budget automatically, so variances are visible without manual comparison.

Forecasting & cashflow

Your cash position is projected forward from live data, not a static monthly spreadsheet.

Client-to-cash workflows

Client onboarding

New client setup is completed from a signed form, without repeated manual paperwork.

Proposal generation

Proposals are created from a standard template and client data, ready to send in minutes.

Contract generation

Contracts are drafted automatically from agreed terms, using your existing templates.

Invoice generation

Invoices are created and sent automatically based on your billing triggers.
